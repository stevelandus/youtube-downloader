Deploying on Render / Cloud Host?

How to bypass YouTube's "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" error

Why does this happen on Render?

YouTube blocks automated traffic coming from cloud hosting IPs (Render, AWS, DigitalOcean). Your local PC works because it's a residential IP.

2-Minute Solution:

1
Export YouTube Cookies

Install the free extension "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" on Chrome or Firefox, open youtube.com, and export your cookies file.

2
Add Variable in Render Dashboard

In your Render Web Service → Environment tab, add a new environment variable:

Key: YOUTUBE_COOKIES
Value: [Paste the complete text from your exported cookies.txt]
3
Save & Re-deploy

Render will automatically restart the server with authenticated YouTube cookies. Both standard videos and live streams will download flawlessly!
